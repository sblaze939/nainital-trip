Skip These
✕
Naina Zoo (High Altitude Zoo)
Average zoo, nothing special at this altitude. Not worth 2 hours of your time.
✕
Eco Cave Garden
Commercial, basic artificial caves. Tourist trap. The ₹75 entry isn't worth it.
✕
Naina Peak (China Peak)
12 km round trip serious trek, 4–5 hours. Snow View Point gives near-identical views in 1/5th the effort by cable car. Skip unless you're avid trekkers.
Hidden Gems
◆
Naukuchiatal
Nine-cornered lake, 30 km from Nainital. Almost zero tourists. Surreally quiet for a Sunday.
◆
Ramgarh Village
25 km from Nainital. "Fruit Bowl of Kumaon" — apple orchards mid-October, orchard views, almost no footfall. Great if you have a spare half-day.
◆
Bal Mithai
Uttarakhand's signature sweet — roasted khoya coated in white sugar balls. Baba Misthan Bhandar on Mall Road makes the best. Buy a 500g box to take home.

Pack for October Nainital
Light jacket or fleece — evenings drop to 10–14°C
Compact rain jacket — light showers possible in early October; folds into a day bag pocket
Comfortable walking shoes — Tiffin Top and temple steps need grip
Cash — many small shops and dhabas don't take UPI reliably in the hills
Power bank — cold weather drains phone batteries fast
Sunscreen — high altitude UV is strong even in October
