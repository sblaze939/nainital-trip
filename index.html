Couple's Trip
Nainital, October 2026
Leave Gurugram 9 Oct night Arrive 10 Oct, 7–8 AM Depart 12 Oct night 3 full days
Skip These
✕
Naina Zoo (High Altitude Zoo)
Average zoo, nothing special. Not worth 2 hours.
✕
Eco Cave Garden
Commercial artificial caves. Tourist trap. Skip.
✕
Naina Peak trek
12 km, 4–5 hrs. Snow View cable car gives same views in 1/5th the effort.
Hidden Gems
◆
Naukuchiatal
Nine-cornered lake, 30 km out. Almost zero tourists even on a Sunday.
◆
Ramgarh Village
25 km away — apple orchards in October, surreally quiet.
◆
Bal Mithai
Kumaon's signature sweet — roasted khoya in white sugar. Baba Misthan Bhandar on Mall Road.

Pack for October Nainital
Light jacket or fleece — evenings drop to 10–14°C
Compact rain jacket — light showers possible; folds into a day bag pocket
Comfortable walking shoes — temple steps and Tiffin Top need grip
Cash — many hill dhabas don't take UPI reliably
Power bank — cold drains phone batteries fast
Sunscreen — high-altitude UV is strong even in October
